## About

I am currently a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by [Prof. Hong Xu](https://henryhxu.github.io/). My research interests lie in AI infrastructure and large-scale distributed training.

I am also a research intern with the Infrastructure team at ByteDance Seed. Previously, I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Xingda Wei](https://ipads.se.sjtu.edu.cn/pub/members/xingda_wei) and [Prof. Haibo Chen](https://ipads.se.sjtu.edu.cn/pub/members/haibo_chen) at the Institute of Parallel and Distributed Systems (IPADS), Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I received my bachelor’s degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

## Publications

### [KunServe: Efficient Parameter-centric Memory Management for LLM Serving](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.18169)

Rongxin Cheng, **Yuxin Lai**, Xingda Wei, Rong Chen, Haibo Chen · *EuroSys 2026*

*KunServe* proposes the first parameter-centric approach to handling throttling by selectively dropping replicated parameters to instantly free memory for requests.
